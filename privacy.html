AcupZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

AcupZ ("we", "our") is a local football and futsal tournament platform operated by ACUPZ PTY LTD, a company registered in Australia. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash), preferred language, and — for Player/Coach accounts — phone number and date of birth.
  • Profile content: photos, team/club details, and sports statistics you or your organisers add.
  • Purchases: subscription status is processed by Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Device data: a push-notification token (only if you allow notifications).

How we use it

  • To operate tournaments, matches, standings and profiles you take part in.
  • To send match and tournament notifications you opt into.
  • To verify accounts and keep the platform safe.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Sharing

Public sports information (display name, team, match statistics) is visible to other users as part of the service. Service providers we rely on: Apple (payments), RevenueCat (subscription management), email delivery for verification codes, and secure cloud storage for images.

Your privacy controls

  • Hide your date of birth, phone, or social links from your public profile (Profile → Privacy).
  • Delete your account and personal data at any time in-app: Settings → Security → Delete Account.

Data retention

Personal data is removed when you delete your account. Anonymised match records may be retained for the integrity of tournament history.

Children

AcupZ is intended for users aged 13 and over.

Contact

Questions or requests: acupz.app@gmail.com or the in-app Support screen.

Data controller: ACUPZ PTY LTD, Australia.
