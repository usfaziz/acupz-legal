Delete your AcupZ account & data

Last updated: June 2026

This page explains how to delete your AcupZ account (published on Google Play and the App Store by ACUPZ PTY LTD, Australia), how to delete some of your data without closing the account, and what happens to your data afterwards.

Option 1 — Delete your account in the app (immediate)

  1. Open AcupZ and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Security.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deactivated immediately and your personal data is deleted as described below.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email acupz.app@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account with the subject “Delete my AcupZ account”. We verify ownership by replying to that address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Profile photo / team logo: open the profile or team page you own → tap the image → Remove photo.
  • Phone number, date of birth, social links: Profile → Edit profile → clear the field → Save.
  • Hide data instead of deleting it: Profile → Privacy lets you hide your date of birth, phone and social links from your public profile.
  • Push-notification token: turn notifications off in Settings → Notifications (or in your device settings) and the token is removed.
  • Followed teams / tournaments: unfollow from the team or tournament page.
  • Anything else: email acupz.app@gmail.com.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhen you delete your account
Name, email, phone number, date of birth, nationality, password hash, language, privacy settings, social linksDeleted immediately
Profile photo, cover photo and images you uploadedDeleted immediately
Push-notification token, device identifiers, approximate location used for filteringDeleted immediately
Subscription (Pro) statusRemoved from AcupZ. Manage or cancel the subscription itself in Google Play → Subscriptions / App Store → Subscriptions; Google / Apple keep purchase records under their own policies.
Match events you appeared in (goals, cards, appearances in approved results)Anonymised and kept — the record stays part of the tournament's results and standings, but is no longer linked to you (“Deleted player”).
Teams, tournaments and matches you organisedKept for the other participants and shown without your personal details. Transfer ownership before deleting if you want someone else to manage them.
Moderation / safety records (reports, warnings, fraud flags)Kept for up to 12 months for security, fraud-prevention and legal reasons, then deleted.
Server backupsPurged automatically within 30 days.
